The best way to reach us is by email: orbitaltraffic@outlook.com. Whether it's a bug, a question about how something works, or feedback on the app, send it our way — we read every message and reply as quickly as we can.

If you're comfortable with GitHub, you're also welcome to open an issue on the project's repository — handy if you'd like to track a bug report or feature request publicly. This is entirely optional; email works just as well for anything.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the app ask for my location?
Only for What's Overhead, which shows which tracked objects are currently passing above you. That calculation runs entirely on your device — your location is never sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Do I need an account to use Orbital Traffic?
No. There's no sign-in, no account, and no sync — everything, including your saved lists, lives only on your own device.
