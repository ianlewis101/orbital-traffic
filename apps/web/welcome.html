Questions

Is Orbital Traffic free?

Completely free. No ads, no subscriptions, no account required.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes — after the first load it works offline, and the catalog refreshes automatically the next time you're online.

Is my location ever sent anywhere?

No. What's Overhead uses your location entirely on your device to work out what's above you — it's never transmitted or stored, by us or anyone else.

What platforms does it run on?

iOS is coming to the App Store — currently in testing. It also installs as a full app on Android and desktop straight from your browser, no app store required.

Where does the data come from?

Real orbital data from CelesTrak, NASA/JPL, and Launch Library 2 — refreshed continuously, not simulated.
